## Spooky Cat Cafe

Welcome to **Spooky Cat Cafe**, a fast-paced, Halloween-themed time-management browser game! Step behind the counter, whip up spooky caffeinated elixirs, and serve hungry monsters before their patience runs out.

---

## Features

* **Spooky Clients:** Serve unique supernatural customers including Vampires, Zombies, Witches, and Pumpkins!
* **Dynamic Recipes:** Mix and match different bases (Coffee, Apple Cider, Hot Chocolate), flavored syrups, ice preferences, and delicious toppings (Whipped Cream, Cinnamon, Marshmallows, Chocolate Sprinkles).
* **Patience Countdown:** Keep an eye on your customer's patience meter—if they wait too long, they'll storm out!
* **Arcade Action:** Race against a 60-second timer to rack up as many coins as possible.
* **Immersive Audio:** Complete with background music, coffee pouring sound effects, and clinking coin rewards.

---

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game:** Open the game in your browser and click the **Start Game** button to begin the 60-second timer.
2. **Read the Order:** Look at the customer's order card on screen to check their specific requirements:
   * **Ice:** Yes or No
   * **Base:** Coffee, Apple Cider, or Hot Chocolate
   * **Syrup:** Pumpkin, Caramel, Cider, or Chocolate Syrup
   * **Toppings:** One or more toppings (Whipped Cream, Cinnamon, Marshmallows, Chocolate Sprinkles)
3. **Build the Drink:** Click the ingredient buttons on your station to build the order. Selected ingredients will highlight. 
   * *Need to start over?* Click the **Clear** button to empty your cup.
4. **Serve:** Once the drink matches the recipe, click the **Serve** button to hand it to the customer, earn **+10 coins**, and welcome the next visitor!
